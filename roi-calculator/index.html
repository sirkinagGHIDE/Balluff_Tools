Step 2

Upfront costs

One-time costs. The current system is usually $0 unless it needs a refresh or replacement anyway.

Net investment is the proposed upfront total minus the current upfront total.

Rolling out to several stations, machines or lines? Enter costs for one here and in step 3, then set the count under Results.

Step 3

Annual operating costs

Enter what each cost runs per year today. The proposed cost follows the reduction %; type a proposed $ value directly to override it.

Not sure of an annual figure? Use the button on a row to work it out from cost per occurrence and frequency.
